Privacy Policy

Last updated November 24, 2026

Overview

Marsh n Main ("we," "us," or "our") operates marshnmain.com. This page explains what information we collect from visitors to this site, how we use it, and how we handle the data behind the tools we use to run the business, including the internal dashboard we use to monitor site performance.

Information collected on this site

Like most business websites, marshnmain.com uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors find and use the site, through standard cookies and similar tracking technology. This tells us things like which pages are visited and how people arrived here. We don't use this information to identify individual visitors personally, and we don't sell it to anyone.

If you submit a contact form, we use the information you provide (such as your name, email, and message) only to respond to your inquiry. We don't add you to a mailing list or share that information with third parties without your permission.

Google Search Console and Analytics data

We use Google Analytics and Google Search Console to monitor the performance of marshnmain.com and the client sites we manage, through a private dashboard tool built for internal use. This tool reads aggregate site performance data we already own, such as traffic counts and search rankings. It does not access or expose any personal information about individual site visitors beyond what Google Analytics itself collects, and that data is never shared outside Marsh n Main.

Third-party services

This site may link to third-party services, such as our social media profiles or scheduling tools. Those services have their own privacy practices, and we encourage you to review them separately.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy or how your information is handled, you can reach us through the contact form at marshnmain.com.
